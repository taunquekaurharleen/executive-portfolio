Supporting modernization of legacy enterprise systems

GreenShield — Senior Business Systems Analyst

The challenge

Legacy applications and operational processes require careful analysis of dependencies, business requirements, technical debt, and risk before modernization work can proceed.

My role

Led discovery and business systems analysis for modernization and operational improvement initiatives, collaborating with business stakeholders, developers, architects, and operational teams.

What I did

  • Documented business processes, system dependencies, technical architecture, and operational workflows.
  • Facilitated requirements discussions to identify current-state challenges and future-state needs.
  • Assessed opportunities for simplification, automation, and maintainability improvements.
  • Supported solution design, prioritization, estimation, and modernization planning.

Business contribution

Helped establish clearer understanding of legacy systems and more structured modernization recommendations, supporting cross-functional planning and operational continuity.

Evidence note: No operational savings, exception reductions, or implementation outcomes are claimed without supporting records and disclosure approval.
