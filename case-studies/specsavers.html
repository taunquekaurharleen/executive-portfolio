Leading cross-functional healthcare technology delivery

Specsavers — Agile Delivery Manager / Agile Specialist

The challenge

Delivering healthcare technology enhancements required coordination across product teams, healthcare stakeholders, engineering, design, and operational support, while balancing interoperability, usability, and release readiness.

My role

Managed a team of eight project managers, analysts, and external consultants, including performance management, coaching, and delivery oversight.

What I did

  • Coordinated delivery across digital identity, doctor referrals, patient management, electronic medical records, and tele-optometry initiatives.
  • Supported EMR enhancement work focused on interoperability and information exchange.
  • Coordinated release planning, change approvals, rollout preparation, and service introduction.
  • Coached teams in Agile delivery practices and supported cross-functional collaboration.

Business contribution

Supported coordinated product delivery, healthcare system enhancements, release readiness, and team development.
