Establishing enterprise transformation governance

Brock University — Associate Director, Enterprise Transformation Office

The challenge

Major university technology initiatives required coordinated governance across business units, technology teams, executives, and vendors. The portfolio included student information systems, identity and access management, CRM, cybersecurity, and other modernization efforts.

My role

Led strategic planning, portfolio governance, procurement coordination, and stakeholder alignment. Managed program-related budgets without final approval authority; supervised one direct report, three dotted-line reports, and coordinated external vendors.

What I did

  • Established and operationalized transformation governance, reporting, and risk-management practices.
  • Supported strategic planning and procurement readiness for the Student Information System modernization program.
  • Coordinated governance and vendor activities supporting Identity and Access Management modernization.
  • Led Architectural Review Board and New Initiative Gating processes involving architecture, security, privacy, and other stakeholders.
  • Developed roadmaps, executive decision-support materials, and cross-functional Centre of Excellence practices.

Business contribution

Strengthened the structures used to coordinate and evaluate strategic technology initiatives, supporting executive decision-making, procurement readiness, and cross-functional alignment.
